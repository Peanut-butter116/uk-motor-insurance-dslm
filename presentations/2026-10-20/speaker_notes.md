## Slide 7

Both historical fine-tuning runs used 21 eligible seed examples. They did not use the new interim production snapshot. We verified full training, saved adapter hashes and successful reload checks against the launch-time code. The Qwen SFT inference had completed on Kaggle but its outputs had not been incorporated in the README. We recovered and verified them. With RAG, Ministral citation resolution increases from 0.813 to 0.925 on the motor subset. Qwen shows a different pattern: parse rate increases from 69.7% to 88.2%, while citation resolution decreases from 0.889 to 0.786. Qwen refuses none of the nine unanswerable motor items in that SFT-plus-RAG arm. This is evidence of mixed behavioural changes. It does not establish an accuracy benefit. A paired bootstrap estimates the Qwen parse improvement at 18.4 percentage points, with an exploratory 95% interval from 7.9 to 28.9 points. Source: verified summary and paired uncertainty report.
